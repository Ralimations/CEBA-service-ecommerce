## Flagged or incomplete proposals

- **Photo evidence:** no secure attachment workflow; see the page 4/10 contradiction above.
- **Cancellation fees, refunds, and compensation:** no amount, schedule, exceptions, or approved external-payment workflow; no monetary assessment is performed.
- **Birthday rewards and cash-valued points:** preview only; no birth date is stored and XP remains nonredeemable.
- **Standalone AI support / live chat:** not integrated. The local FAQ assistant, platform ticketing, and customer-provider messages remain separate.
- **Camera QR scanning:** manual code entry remains the accessible fallback and only implemented interaction.
- **Collateral/capital:** future research requiring a separate product, legal, and finance review.
- **Competitor concepts:** not acceptance criteria for this sprint.
